TECHNIQUES EST. 1994 // KANAGAWA

01 // CLUTCH KICK

INSTANT OVERSTEER

The most aggressive way to initiate. By rapidly disengaging and re-engaging the clutch while at high RPM, you send a sudden shock of torque to the rear wheels, breaking traction instantly.

bolt HIGH INTENSITY
speed REQ: 4500+ RPM
02 // E-BRAKE SLIDE

THE PRECISION LINE

Used to extend a drift or adjust the angle mid-corner. Pulling the handbrake locks the rear wheels, allowing the car's momentum to carry the rear end out while keeping the front wheels pointed toward the apex.

"DON'T FORGET TO DEPRESS THE CLUTCH OR YOU'LL STALL THE ENGINE MID-SLIDE!"
03 // POWER OVER

RAW HORSEPOWER

Entering a turn and simply pinning the throttle to overwhelm the tires. This requires significant torque and perfect countersteering to prevent a full spin-out.

Steering COUNTER
Throttle 100%
